## Recommended arm (`w1-S-recommended.args`, SHARED_W1 rev. 1)

Same as the shared arm except loss and advantage at miles' recipe defaults. Async/off-policy, batching and zero-advantage handling are already at the recipe defaults in the shared arm (fully async, TIS, buffer 2x, no staleness filter, no dynamic sampling), so they don't change.

| knob             | shared                                                  | recommended (miles default)                                              |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| advantage        | group mean only (`--disable-grpo-std-normalization`)    | `(r - mean) / (std + eps)` per group (GRPO default)                      |
| loss aggregation | token mean over the step (`--calculate-per-token-loss`) | per-sample token mean, averaged over the step's rollouts (miles default) |

Kept shared even though the recipe differs, since SHARED_W1 says everything other than loss/advantage/async/batching stays shared: optimizer (recipe: wd 0.1, betas 0.9/0.98), the zero-coefficient KL loss the recipe loads a reference model for (no effect on the loss), and the attention backend workaround.

## Gaps

- No min_p knob exposed by miles' rollout args; SGLang's default (0) is what SHARED_W1 asks for anyway.
- The PPO ratio's denominator is the trainer's recomputed logprob, with TIS for the rollout/trainer gap. A library that uses the rollout logprobs directly as `pi_old` gets a different clipping region under staleness. Recorded, not changed: TIS is miles' recommended async setting.
- Prompts over 1024 tokens are dropped rather than truncated.
